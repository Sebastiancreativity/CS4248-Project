What is the “Fund E Account” APP?
According to the relevant documents, the Fund's E Account APP is a one-stop shop application for China's clearing and development of a PFP industry portal approved by the CSRC to provide investors with off-site shares of PGF products.
Investors can improve the efficiency of their inquiries across the board by facilitating and fast-tracking the holding of off-site shares of PGF products purchased by their respective fund managers through the sales channels of the market as a whole.
