Students take center stage at Zhejiang University Academic Year, research results open up new horizons - Xinhuanet
Washing hands with air, breathing out to judge diseases, and precisely searching for the source of smog. A series of academic achievements with great imagination were awarded at the opening ceremony of the 2016 Academic Year Conference of Zhejiang University yesterday evening.
The main researchers of these achievements are students, and the research covers environmental, mechanical, energy, medical, life science, humanities education and other major fields. The students started from a curious idea and created many high-end inventions with application value, some of which have won awards at home and abroad.
Biological sensors: your home health check doctor
The team of Zhang Diming, a doctoral student in the School of Biomedical Engineering, demonstrated a smartphone-based biosensor at the site, which is half the size of a smartphone.
"You just need to breathe gently and this instrument can detect your health condition from the gas components in your breath. "
Zhang Diming said that this sensor is connected to the smartphone. After exhaling, the health report will be displayed on the phone.
Smart bracelets on the market that can detect health conditions generally detect physical parameters such as temperature and pulse.
This biosensor uses graphene as a new material and its target is chemical elements. Its keen "sense of smell" allows it to more deeply and accurately reflect the health status.
Smart surgical lights: doctors' high-energy eyes
In addition to smartphones, smart surgical lights have also been used in operating rooms.
This invention won the silver medal at the 44th Geneva International Exhibition of Inventions in Switzerland this year.
The master’s student Zhu Linjing and Shen Junfei team of the School of Optoelectronic Science and Engineering opened up new ideas in the defects of traditional surgical lamps, and invented smart LED surgical lamp.
"Many diseases early detection and removal are not in place, which is related to the light used by doctors in the operating room. "
Shen Junfei said that for example, when cancer is detected early, if doctors cannot see all tumor tissues in time, then cancer cells will still spread.
The smart surgical light has four lamp heads and can enhance the distinction of different tissues under illumination through adjusting the spectrum.
During surgery, doctors can adjust the size of the spot, brightness, spectrum, color uniformity and shadow rate according to their needs and personal habits.

Last October, this invention won the first prize of National Challenge Cup.

Only then comes the trial and punishment of the suspect.
The significance of criminal punishment can be listed as many "big words" from individual punishment, social prevention to legal education.
However, before the trafficking case is solved, priority should be given to the personal safety of the victims.
Things have their priorities. In the urgent "rescue" purpose, topics such as "whether the death penalty should be imposed on all cases of child trafficking" can be put aside for cautious discussion; but the rescue of trafficked children cannot wait.

The International Olive Council classifies olive oil into two major categories, virgin olive oil and lampante olive oil or refined olive oil, with five levels.
Virgin olive oil or natural olive oil is the oil obtained directly from fresh olives through mechanical cold pressing and filtration to remove impurities.
It is divided into three levels according to the acidity:
Extra Virgin Olive Oil: This is the highest grade and best quality olive oil, which is a pure natural product.
It has a great taste with a delicate and pleasant plant fragrance, and an acid content of less than 1%.
Fine virgin olive oil: Slightly higher acidity, but not more than 2%, pure and aromatic taste.
Ordinary Virgin: The taste and flavor are still acceptable, with an acid content not exceeding 3.3%.
Refined olive oil is the olive oil obtained from the refining of virgin olive oil with an acid content above 3.3%, or "second oil".
There are two grades of refined olive oil:
Olive Oil: Refined olive oil mixed with a certain proportion of virgin olive oil to blend taste and color, its acidity is less than 1.5%, and it is transparent light yellow.
Refined olive-pomace oil: is extracted from the pomace by dissolution and refined to obtain olive oil.
Having seen the grading system, you should have noticed that acidity is a very important factor in olive oil.
It is known that olives are like grapes used to make wine. Olives from different regions and different olive varieties will produce olive oil of different standards.
The best growing place for olive oil:
The Mediterranean coast has the most suitable soil and climate conditions for olive trees, including temperature, sunshine, air humidity and rainfall.
Outside of the Mediterranean coast, it can only be planted sporadically in suitable areas.
Main production areas (Mediterranean):
Italy, Spain, Greece, Turkey, Portugal, Morocco, Syria, Israel, Tunisia, Algeria, Egypt, Pakistan, Lebanon and so on, as well as those with similar climates in the southern hemisphere such as Argentina, Peru, South Africa and so on.
Olive harvest month:
From March to June every year is the time for olive trees to flower and bear fruit. From July to October, the fruits begin to develop and mature, changing from green to red, purple, and finally black.
The fruit is harvested from November to February (olive oil freshness is very important).
Nutritional value of olive oil:
Olive oil is rich in monounsaturated fatty acids, such as oleic acid and linoleic acid, linolenic acid, vitamin A, vitamin B, vitamin D, vitamin E, vitamin K and antioxidants.
Olive oil is considered to be the most suitable oil for human nutrition among all oils discovered so far.

Educational Thinking: How can university exams meet the needs of "top students" and "underachievers"? - Xinhuanet
Three students of Lanzhou Jiaotong University wrote letters to the school leaders, reflecting their confusion about university exams and proposed to increase the difficulty of the exam, change the type of questions, and make the exam truly reflect the actual level of students.
Indeed, university exams have long been a "hot topic".
In university campus, students come from all over the world and have different levels of ability. In addition, the level of study is also different, so the situation of learning is very different.
It is inevitable that there will be different feelings when evaluating such a group with so many diverse characteristics.
Of course, if the test questions are monotonous and repetitive, and the questions are too simple, it will inevitably cause another completely different feeling.
Students who do not work hard may be able to pass the exam by cramming before the exam. If they try other methods, it is possible that they will achieve twice the result with half the effort.
In this way, there will be "dull students" who can pass without putting in much effort and even get high scores; and "top students" who never miss classes, have good homework and notes, but do not score much higher on exams.
It seems that the students of Lanzhou Jiaotong University reflect on the problems existing in university examinations are not without reason, and it is believed that such conditions do not exist only at Lanzhou Jiaotong University.
From this perspective, we should give a thumbs up to these three students who wrote letters directly.
In fact, those who have attended university know that it is not uncommon to rush to prepare for exams and try every means to "extract" test questions from teachers, seek to define the scope of the exam, and seek "intensive guidance" before the exam. Even carrying cheat sheets in the examination and asking teachers to be lenient after the exam are not rare.
In my view, as an orientation for teaching quality assessment, whether a university examination is qualified or not should be measured in terms of teachers’ comprehensive grasp of teaching and learning, as well as scientific questions based on this.
When entering university, majors begin to be divided and many professional courses are based on basic knowledge.
It is beyond doubt that whether to fully master the basic knowledge points of a subject is what university examinations will test.
Undergraduate knowledge is basically based on basic theories and common sense of disciplines. From the perspective of knowledge points, there is not much change, which is basically fixed.
As teachers said, "the basic knowledge of university mathematics learned 20 years ago is still being studied and is still the focus."
However, the basic knowledge points remain unchanged, which does not mean that the test types are single and the questions are repeated.
In many universities, exams are generally composed of a group of examiners, and then compiled by senior teachers in charge of the subject, and the examination is also reviewed by all teachers responsible for the subject, which can overcome the problem of repeated questions to some extent.
However, to draft a set of test questions that fit the actual teaching and learning, the difficulty level of the questions should be normally distributed according to the overall situation of students’ learning, and the teaching situation should be comprehensively grasped, and the degree of student’s learning should be comprehensively analyzed so that the examination questions can have dynamic control over the difficulty level and question types, which can highlight the basic knowledge examination, take into account the ability examination, and at the same time innovate the design of question types.
Exams are the command stick of university education. What kind of exams there are, students will have corresponding preparation plans. In the long run, this will affect the effectiveness of university education.
For the suggestions letter of students, Lanzhou Jiaotong University attaches great importance to it and has deployed a rectification plan throughout the university.
It is required that each teaching unit organize teachers to carry out seminars and self-examination of course final examination, formulate examination papers, related systems, and strengthen supervision and inspection of the examination paper formulation and review process, refine the assessment, and strengthen the comprehensive assessment of all links in the course teaching.
From the mechanism to solve the problems of single test types and repeated questions is an issue that universities should face. Of course, relevant systems need to be introduced, but it is important to do things on a daily basis. Teachers need to have a comprehensive understanding of teaching conditions, not only to understand the teaching content, but also to understand students' learning conditions. Both ends must be understood, and teaching and learning will better match, forming a benign interaction, and exams will be more in line with reality, so that "geniuses" and "dunces" can each find their place.

Xinhua News Agency, Xining, December 11 (Reporters Luo Xiaofei and Zhang Hongxiang) -- According to the reporter from Qinghai Provincial Department of Agriculture and Rural Affairs, the latest monitoring results show that the resource stock of Qinghai Lake naked carp has increased by 0.71 thousand tons this year compared with last year, reaching 108,500 tons, which is more than 40 times the initial protection in 2002.
The Qinghai Lake naked carp is commonly known as "Huangyue". It is a unique and precious fish species in Qinghai Lake. It plays an important role in maintaining the safety of the "water-fish-bird" ecological chain in the Qinghai Lake basin and the biodiversity.
In the 1960s and 1970s, due to the large-scale hunting of naked carp and the increasingly deteriorating environment of the spawning grounds, the number of Qinghai Lake naked carp sharply declined, and in 2002, the resource reserve of Qinghai Lake naked carp was only 2,592 tons.
In 2004, the Qinghai Lake Naked Carp was listed as an endangered species in the Red List of Chinese Species.
Since 2003, Qinghai Province has implemented the ban on fishing in Qinghai Lake and its main rivers and tributaries, prohibiting any unit, collective or individual from fishing for naked carp in Qinghai Lake and its main rivers and tributaries, and prohibiting the sale of naked carp and its products.
Meanwhile, through artificial reproduction and release, cleaning the main river channels of Qinghai Lake, designing scientific and reasonable "passage channels", and other measures to continue to increase protection efforts, the resource reserves of Qinghai Lake naked carp have increased year by year.
According to Yang Lin, deputy director of Qinghai Provincial Department of Agriculture and Rural Affairs, as of now, the number of artificially bred and released Qinghai Lake naked carp has reached nearly 180 million, and its contribution rate to the recovery of Qinghai Lake naked carp resources is more than 23%.
Qinghai Sanjiangyuan is an important ecological barrier and water source conservation area in China and Asia. Qinghai Lake is a natural barrier and ecological function zone to control the western desertification from spreading eastward.

The Yangtze River mainstream has achieved full coverage of water-based green comprehensive service areas - Xinhuanet
Xinhua News Agency, Wuhan, December 11 (Reporter Wang Xian) - The Chongqing Fuling Water Green Comprehensive Service Area was built and put into operation on schedule on October 10. This is the second water green comprehensive service area in the upper reaches of the Yangtze River to be officially put into operation after the water green comprehensive service area in Luzhou, Sichuan Province was put into operation on August 8.
Up to now, the water-based green comprehensive service area has covered the upper, middle and lower reaches of the Yangtze River.
Qiu Jianhua, deputy director of the Yangtze River Maritime Administration of the Ministry of Transport, said that previously, 10 water-based green comprehensive service areas had been built and put into operation in the middle and lower reaches of the Yangtze River in Hubei, Hunan, Jiangxi, Anhui, and Jiangsu provinces, which had received recognition from a wide range of crew members and played an important role in promoting high-quality development of the Yangtze River shipping industry.

It is reported that the water-based green comprehensive service area provides all-round production and living services for ships and crew members, and helps to solve the "urgent, difficult, anxious and worried" problems of the vast majority of crew members.
The two upstream water green integrated service areas built and put into operation this time integrate life services, production services and government services, take environmental protection as the theme, and take ship repair and emergency rescue services as the feature to achieve "one-time berthing and multiple services" for ships in port, which will make the work of crew members who have been sailing on the Yangtze River more efficient and convenient.

Article 17 The state implements a registration practice management system for registered fire protection engineers.
Those who have obtained the first and second level registered fire engineer qualification certificate can practice in the name of corresponding level registered fire engineers after registration.
Article 18 The Fire Bureau of Ministry of Public Security is the registration approval department for first-class registered fire engineers.
The provincial, autonomous region and municipal public security fire institutions are the registration approval departments for second-level registered fire engineers, and they are responsible for preliminary review of first-level registered fire engineers.
Article 19 Those who have obtained the first and second level registered fire engineer qualification certificate and apply for registration shall be employed by an approved fire service institution or key unit of fire safety, and submit the registration application materials to the local public security fire department (for enterprises, through the enterprise to the industrial and commercial registration location) through the employer.
Article 20 After receiving the registration application materials of the applicant, the provincial, autonomous region and municipal public security fire institutions shall inform the applicant in writing of all contents that need to be corrected at one time on the spot or within five working days if the application materials are incomplete or do not conform to the statutory form. If it does not inform the applicant in writing, it shall be deemed to have been accepted from the date of receipt of the application materials.
For the registration applications accepted or not accepted, written certificates with the official seal of the fire control institution and the date should be issued.
Article 21 The provincial, autonomous region and municipal public security fire institutions shall complete the preliminary review work of the first-level registered fire engineer qualification registration application materials and the registration approval work of the second-level registered fire engineer qualification within 20 working days from the date of acceptance of the registration application in accordance with the prescribed conditions and procedures, and report the first-level registered fire engineer qualification registration application materials and preliminary review opinions to the Fire Bureau of the Ministry of Public Security for approval.
The Fire Bureau of Ministry of Public Security shall make a decision on whether to approve within 20 working days from the date of receipt of the application materials and preliminary review opinions submitted by the provincial public security fire institutions.
If the approval decision cannot be made within the prescribed time limit, the extension of the time limit and reasons shall be informed to the applicant.
For those who make a decision of non-approval, they should write down reasons and inform the applicant of their right to apply for administrative reconsideration or initiate an administrative lawsuit according to law.
Article 22 The registration approval department shall issue and deliver the corresponding level of registration certificate within 10 working days from the date of making the approval decision.
Article 23 The registration certificate is valid for three years.
The registration certificate is the practice credential of the corresponding level registered fire engineer during its validity period, and shall be kept and used by the registered fire engineer himself.
Article 24: Those who apply for initial registration shall submit their application within one year from the date of obtaining the qualification certificate of first and second level registered fire engineers.
When applying for initial registration late, it must meet the continuing education requirements of this regulation.
The specific methods for initial registration, continuation registration, change registration, cancellation registration and non-registration shall be prescribed by the Ministry of Public Security.
Article 25 Continuing education is a necessary condition for the continuation registration, re-registration and overdue initial registration of registered fire engineers.
During each registration period, registered fire engineers at all levels shall complete corresponding continuing education in accordance with regulations.
Article 26 The registration approval department shall promptly announce to the public the relevant circumstances of the registration of registered fire protection engineers and establish an integrity file for registered fire protection engineers to implement credit management on their professional activities.
Article 27 The registration management department of registered fire engineers at all levels shall strictly implement the relevant provisions of the Administrative Licensing Law of the People's Republic of China.

The "Peace Rescue - 2021" Joint Medical Exercise of the Chinese and Vietnamese Armies was successfully concluded - Xinhuanet
Xinhua News Agency, Hanoi, December 12 (Zhou Na, Teng Hua Jin) - All Chinese participating units of the "Peace Rescue-2021" joint military exercise between China and Vietnam returned to China's Guangxi Province in the morning of December 12, marking the successful completion of this seven-day training mission.
In this joint exercise, the Chinese and Vietnamese armies focused on humanitarian medical rescue as a major topic, adhered to the mission of saving lives in war-time, and completed the joint medical command, joint field rescue, and mass casualty treatment according to the standard of practical combat, effectively enhancing the medical support capability of the two armies.
The exercise results have been recognized by both the Chinese and Vietnamese military.
Nguyen Xong Thang, Director of the Vietnamese Exercise Guidance Committee and Deputy Chief of Staff of the Logistics Bureau of the People's Army of Vietnam, said at the end of the exercise ceremony that through this exercise, the Vietnamese military doctors learned about the organization and professional activities from the Chinese military doctors, and also saw the medical and support equipment of China.
The results of the exercise proved the professional ability of the two countries' medical forces in medical assistance and epidemic prevention and control.
Chen Chunming, head of the guidance coordination group and deputy director of the Health Bureau of the Central Military Commission's Logistics Support Department said that this exercise was another pioneering practice based on the successful joint medical mission in 2018, which had very important milestone significance.
During the exercise, the People's Liberation Army donated medical protective masks, medical protective clothing, rapid nucleic acid testing instruments and other health and epidemic prevention supplies to the Vietnam People's Army.
