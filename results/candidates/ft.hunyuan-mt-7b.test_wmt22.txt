This invention won the silver award at the 44th Geneva International Invention Exhibition in Switzerland this year.

Many doctors and medical experts in South Africa believe that the symptoms of the Omicron variant of the novel coronavirus are less severe than those of the Delta variant, although it spreads faster.

Dr Unben Pillay, the organisation's director, said he sees dozens of patients every day but none of them have been hospitalised.

He said this includes elderly patients and those with underlying health conditions, who are more likely to become seriously ill from the coronavirus infection.
He said that in comparison, during the last wave of Delta infections, he treated COVID-19 patients who "were struggling to breathe and had low oxygen levels.
Many people will be hospitalized in a few days."
Similar experiences have been shared by other doctors within two weeks of the first report of the Omicron variant in southern Africa.
While it will take weeks to collect enough data, doctors' observations and early evidence are providing some clues.
According to the National Institute for Communicable Diseases of South Africa, among the COVID-19 patients hospitalized in recent weeks, only 30% were severe cases, which is half of the proportion of severe cases in the previous wave; the average length of hospital stay was shortened from 8 days to 2.8 days; among the recent COVID-19 patients, only 3% died, while the death rate in the early outbreak in South Africa reached 20%.

The International Olive Oil Council classifies olive oil into two major categories, virgin olive oil and lampante olive oil or refined olive oil, with five levels.
Extra virgin olive oil, also known as natural olive oil, is a kind of oil obtained by mechanical cold pressing directly from fresh olives and removing foreign matter after filtration.
They are divided into three levels according to acidity:
Extra Virgin Olive Oil: It is the highest grade and highest quality olive oil, which is a natural product.
It has a great taste, with delicate and pleasant plant fragrance, acidity not exceeding 1%.
Fine Virgin: Slightly higher acidity, but not more than 2%, pure and fragrant.
Ordinary Virgin: It has a good taste and flavor, with acidity not exceeding 3.3%.
Refined olive oil refers to the refined extra virgin olive oil with acidity exceeding 3.3%, or "secondary oil".
Refined olive oil is divided into two grades:
Olive Oil: Refined olive oil mixed with a certain proportion of extra virgin olive oil to blend the taste and color, with acidity below 1.5%, is transparent light golden yellow.
Refined Olive-Pomace Oil: It is extracted from oil residue by dissolution and refined.
If you've read the classification, you should have found that the acidity of olive oil is a very important indicator.
It should be noted that olives are like grapes used to make wine, and different standards of olive oil will be produced from different regions and different varieties of olives.
The best place to grow olive oil:
The Mediterranean coast has the most suitable soil and climate conditions such as temperature, sunshine, air humidity and precipitation required for the growth of oil olives.
Except for the Mediterranean coast, other areas can only be sporadically planted in suitable areas.
Main production areas (Mediterranean):
Italy, Spain, Greece, Turkey, Portugal, Morocco, Syria, Israel, Tunisia, Algeria, Egypt, Pakistan, Lebanon, and so on, and similar climates are found in Argentina, Peru, South Africa, and so on, far south of the equator.
Picking months of oil olive:
From March to June every year, it is the time for the olive tree to bloom and bear fruit. From July to October, the fruits begin to develop and mature, and the fruits transition from green to red, purple, and finally black.
The fruit is picked from November to February of the next year (the freshness of olive oil is very important).
Nutritional composition of olive oil:
Olive oil is rich in monounsaturated fatty acids, i.e., oleic acid and linoleic acid, linolenic acid, as well as vitamin A, vitamin B, vitamin D, vitamin E, vitamin K and antioxidants.
Olive oil is considered to be the most suitable oil for human nutrition among all the oils discovered so far.

After the first-instance judgment, the plaintiff filed an appeal.

Educational Thoughts: How Can University Examinations Help "Top Students" and "Poor Students" Find Their Places - Xinhuanet
Three students from Lanzhou Jiaotong University wrote a letter to the school leaders, reflecting their confusion about the university exams and asking for an increase in the difficulty of the exams, changing the types of questions, and making the exams truly reflect the actual level of students.
Indeed, for a long time, university entrance examination has been a “hot topic”.
In the university campus, students come from all over the world, with different levels of ability and learning degrees, so the learning situation is very different.
It is inevitable that there are different feelings about assessing a group with such diverse characteristics.
Of course, if the exam questions are of a single type, with high repetition rates and too simple, it will inevitably cause a very different feeling.
Students who don’t study hard may pass the exam by cramming before the exam, and if they try other methods, they may achieve twice the result with half the effort.
As a result, there is a "strange situation" that "poor students" can pass and even get high scores without investment; "top students" never miss classes, do homework and take notes well, but don't get much higher scores in exams.
In this regard, the problems reflected by Lanzhou Jiaotong University students in the university examination are not without reason. It is believed that this situation does not exist only in Lanzhou Jiaotong University.
In this sense, we should give a thumbs up to these three students who wrote a letter.
In fact, those who have attended university know that cramming for exams, trying various ways to “get” exam questions from teachers, striving to draw the scope of the exam, and seeking “key guidance” before the exam are not uncommon, and even bringing in cheat sheets during the exam and asking teachers to be lenient after the exam are not strange.
In my opinion, as a quality assessment orientation, whether a university passes an examination depends more on teachers' overall grasp of teaching and learning, and scientific proposition based on this.
In the undergraduate stage, the majors begin to be subdivided, and many professional courses are based on basic knowledge.
Undoubtedly, whether a student has fully grasped the basic knowledge points of a subject is what university exams are designed to test.
Undergraduate knowledge is basically basic theory and common sense of disciplines, and there is not much room for change in terms of knowledge points, which are basically fixed.
As a teacher said, "The basic knowledge of college mathematics learned 20 years ago is still being studied now, and it is still the key point. "
However, the fact that the knowledge points remain basically unchanged does not mean that the exam questions are of a single type and the test questions are repeated.
In many universities, the examination is generally composed of a question group, and then the senior teachers of the subject will make up the test paper. The marking of the examination will also be participated by all teachers who are responsible for the subject, so that the repetition of the test questions can be overcome to a certain extent.
However, to draw up a set of test questions that are in line with the actual situation of teaching and learning, the questions should be based on the overall situation of students' learning, and the difficulty should be normally distributed. It is also necessary to comprehensively master the teaching situation and comprehensively analyze the degree of students' learning, so that the examination proposition has dynamic control over the difficulty and question types, which can not only highlight the basic knowledge inspection, but also take into account the ability inspection, and at the same time innovate the question types.
Examination is the baton of university education. What kind of examination students have, they will have corresponding preparation plans. In the long run, it will affect the effectiveness of university education.
Lanzhou Jiaotong University has attached great importance to the letter of advice from students and has deployed a rectification plan throughout the university.
It requires all teaching units to organize teachers to carry out discussions and self-examinations of course final exams, formulate examination papers and related systems, strengthen the supervision and inspection of examination paper proposition and review, refine the assessment of grades, and strengthen the comprehensive assessment of all links in course teaching.
Mechanically solving problems such as single question types and repeated questions is an issue that university examination affairs need to face. Of course, corresponding systems should be introduced, but the key lies in daily efforts. Teachers need to have a comprehensive grasp of teaching situations. They should not only thoroughly understand the teaching content, but also thoroughly understand the students' learning situation. With thorough understanding of both sides, teaching and learning will be better connected, forming a positive interaction, and the examination will be more in line with reality, so that "top students" and "poor students" can get what they deserve.

The resource reserve of Qinghai Lake naked fish increased by 7,100 tons this year - Xinhuanet
Xinhua News Agency, Xining, December 11 (Reporters Luo Xiaofei and Zhang Hongxiang) The latest monitoring results show that the resource reserves of naked carp in Qinghai Lake increased by 7,100 tons this year, reaching 108,500 tons, which is more than 40 times higher than at the beginning of protection in 2002.
The naked carp in Qinghai Lake, commonly known as “Huang fish”, is a unique and precious fish species in Qinghai Lake, which plays a crucial role in maintaining the safety of the “water-fish-bird” ecological chain and biodiversity in the Qinghai Lake basin.
In the 1960s and 1970s, due to factors such as massive human hunting and the increasingly degraded environment of the spawning waters, the number of naked carp in Qinghai Lake decreased sharply. In 2002, the resource reserve of naked carp in Qinghai Lake was only 2,592 tons.
In 2004, Qinghai Lake naked fish was listed as an endangered species in the "China Species Red List".
Since 2003, Qinghai Province has implemented the lake fishery and prohibited any unit, collective and individual to fish for naked carp in Qinghai Lake and the main rivers and tributaries of the lake area, and prohibited the sale of naked carp and its products.
Meanwhile, through artificial propagation and release, cleaning up the main rivers of Qinghai Lake, designing scientific and reasonable "fish passage" and other measures to continue to increase protection efforts, the resource reserves of naked carp in Qinghai Lake have increased year by year.
Yang Lin, deputy director of Qinghai Provincial Department of Agriculture and Rural Affairs, said that as of now, nearly 180 million naked carp have been released in Qinghai Lake, contributing more than 23% to the recovery of naked carp resources in Qinghai Lake.
The Sanjiangyuan region in Qinghai is an important ecological barrier and water conservation area in China and even Asia, while Qinghai Lake is a natural barrier and ecological function area to control the eastward spread of desertification in the west.
In order to protect the ecological environment of the Sanjiangyuan region and Qinghai Lake, protect aquatic wildlife, and maintain the biodiversity of the Qinghai-Tibet Plateau, Qinghai has successively issued notices on the ban of fishing in key waters of the Yangtze River, Yellow River and Qinghai Lake since this year, and has established a comprehensive management system for the ban of fishing in "one river, one river and one lake".

Facebook, Instagram, WhatsApp and so on, whatever their feature orientation, are Meta's traffic channels.
Moreover, Meta is also the largest “ad alliance”. Many apps connect to Meta’s ads and monetize through Meta’s ad platform.
But times have changed.
First, because of the adjustment of Apple's privacy policy, Meta has difficulty in getting user data information, which affects the efficiency of advertising placement.
Apple's current privacy policy is user-friendly, and it will clearly prompt users whether they allow the app to get their information, and most people's normal reaction is definitely not to allow.
If Google were to take another hit, it would have a bigger impact on Meta's revenue.
Second, the China factor in Meta’s revenue growth has also weakened (the proportion of this factor is uncertain how high, but it does exist).
Everyone knows that Meta has no business in China, but this understanding is not accurate.
At least 10% of Meta’s revenue comes from China every year, mainly contributed by companies that have gone overseas.
Meta has about a few hundred people in its Hong Kong office, and several major agents on the mainland.
Meta is an advertising channel that Chinese companies cannot bypass if they want to expand overseas markets, and most of the money burned was given to Meta.
Interestingly, Byte's TT is now one of Meta's main competitors, but Meta made a lot of money from Byte in the past two years.
All domestic agents are helping ByteDance to place advertisements on Meta, and Meta’s performance in Greater China is also very good.
I haven't looked at it recently, but I'm sure it's not any better than before.
The stage of Chinese companies burning money overseas on a large scale has passed.
Third, new traffic channels are not expanding smoothly.
Facebook is getting old, and new platforms including TT are not in Meta's hands.
The biggest problem is not just the decline in income, but the lack of growth expectations.
The capital market does not only focus on income, but more importantly, it focuses on future expectations.
It's clear Meta's expectations weren't enough.
