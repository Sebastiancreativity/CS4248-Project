Extra Virgin: is the highest quality olive oil and is a pure natural product.
The flavor is excellent, with a flavorful vegetable flavor and acidity of no more than 1%.
Fine Virgin olive oil: slightly acidic, but no more than 2%, pure in taste and aroma.
Ordinary Virgin Olive Oil (Ordinary Virgin): Flavor and flavor are still available, with an acidity of no more than 3.3%.
Refined olive oil refers to olive oil obtained from the refining of primary olive oil with an acidity of more than 3.3%, or converted into secondary olive oil.
The refining of olive oil can be divided into two levels:
Ordinary olive oil (Olive Oil): refined olive oil mixed with a certain proportion of primary olive oil to match flavor and colour, with an acidity of less than 1.5% and a transparent pale yellow.
Refined Olive-Pomace Oil: is an olive oil extracted and refined from the oil residue by dissolving it.
