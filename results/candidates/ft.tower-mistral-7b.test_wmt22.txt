Secondly, the trial and punishment of the suspects.

However, before the case of abduction is solved, the priority should be given to the safety of the victim.

The International Olive Oil Association divides olive oil into two main categories, Virgin Olive Oil and Lampante Olive Oil or Refined Olive Oil, with five grades.
Virgin olive oil, also known as natural olive oil, is the oil obtained directly from fresh olives by mechanical cold pressing and then filtered to remove impurities.
They are divided into three levels according to their acidity:
Extra Virgin: The highest quality olive oil, it is a natural product.
It has a great taste, a mild and pleasant plant aroma, and an acidity of less than 1%.
Fine Virgin: slightly more acidic, but not more than 2%, pure and fragrant.
Ordinary Virgin: taste and flavor are acceptable, acidity not exceeding 3.3%.
Refined olive oil is olive oil that has been refined from virgin olive oil with an acidity level greater than 3.3% or becomes "second oil".
Refined olive oil comes in two grades:
Olive Oil: Refined olive oil mixed with a certain percentage of virgin olive oil to moderate the taste and color, with an acidity of less than 1.5% and a transparent pale golden color.
Refined Olive-Pomace Oil: Olive oil obtained by solvent extraction from olive pomace and refined.
You should have noticed by now that the acidity of olive oil is a very important indicator, as per the classification.
You see, olives are like grapes for wine, and olives from different regions and different varieties of olives will produce olive oil of different standards.
Best places to grow olives:
The Mediterranean coast has the most suitable soil and temperature, sunshine, humidity and precipitation for the growth of olive trees.
Except for the Mediterranean coast, it can only be grown in suitable areas.
Main production areas (Mediterranean):
Italy, Spain, Greece, Turkey, Portugal, Morocco, Syria, Israel, Tunisia, Algeria, Egypt, Pakistan, Lebanon, and so on, and those with similar climate in the far south latitude, such as Argentina, Peru, South Africa and so on.
Olive harvesting month:
The time of flowering and fruiting of the olive tree is between March and June each year. From July to October, the fruit begins to develop and mature. The fruit changes from green to red, purple and finally black.
November to February is the harvest season (freshness of olive oil is important).
Nutritional components of olive oil:
Olive oil is rich in a variety of monounsaturated fatty acids, namely oleic acid and linoleic acid, as well as vitamins A, B, D, E, K and antioxidants.
Olive oil is considered to be the most suitable oil for human nutrition among all oils discovered so far.

Education Thought: How University Examinations Can Make “Students with Excellent Academic Performance” and “Students with Poor Academic Performance” Each Get What They Deserve - Xinhuanet
Three students from Lanzhou University of Technology wrote a letter to the university leaders, reflecting their confusion about the university exams, and proposed to increase the difficulty of the exams, change the types of exams, and make the exams truly reflect the actual level of students.
Indeed, for a long time, university exams have been a “hot topic”.
In a university campus, students come from all over the world, with different levels of ability, and with varying degrees of study, so the learning situation is very different.
There are bound to be different feelings about assessing a group with so many diverse characteristics.
Of course, if the exam questions are single, repetitive and too simple, it will inevitably cause another kind of feeling.
Students who do not work hard may pass the exam after a concentrated attack before the exam. If you want to try other methods, you may achieve half the work with double the result.
In this way, the "slacker" can pass the exam and even get high scores without putting in effort; the "studious student" never misses class, and the homework and notes are all good, but the exam is not much higher than the average.
It seems that the students of Lanzhou Jiaotong University reflect the problems in the university exams, which is not groundless. It is believed that this situation is not only in Lanzhou Jiaotong University.
In this sense, we should applaud the three students who wrote the letter.
In fact, people who have been to college know that the phenomenon of cramming, trying all kinds of ways to "get" questions from teachers, trying to get the scope of the exam, and trying to get "key guidance" before the exam is not uncommon. Even the situation of taking notes during the exam and asking teachers for help after the exam is not uncommon.
In the author’s view, as a teaching quality assessment guideline, the merit of a university’s exams is more dependent on the teacher’s overall grasp of teaching and learning, and the scientific formulation of questions based on this.
When entering the undergraduate stage, the majors begin to be subdivided, and many majors are mainly based on basic knowledge.
It is undoubtedly the basic knowledge points of a discipline whether to fully master, which is the content to be examined in the university exam.
And the knowledge of undergraduate is basically the basic theory and common sense of the discipline. From the perspective of knowledge, the space of change is not large, and it is basically fixed.
As one teacher said, "the basic knowledge of mathematics in college 20 years ago is still being studied, and it is still a key. "
However, the knowledge points are basically unchanged, which does not mean that the types of exams are single and the questions are repeated.
In many universities, the examination will generally be composed of a group of examiners, and then the senior teachers of the discipline will organize the test paper. The examination will also be reviewed by all the teachers in charge of the subject, so that the situation of repetition of the test can be overcome to a certain extent.
However, in order to come up with a set of questions that are in line with the actual teaching and learning, the questions should be based on the overall learning situation of the students, and should be distributed in a normal distribution. It is also necessary to comprehensively master the teaching situation and comprehensively analyze the degree of learning of the students, so that the questions of the examination can have a dynamic grasp of the difficulty and types of questions, so that the examination can highlight the examination of basic knowledge and also take into account the examination of ability, and at the same time, the types of questions can be designed in an innovative way.
Examinations are the baton of university education. Whatever kind of examinations there are, students will have corresponding preparation plans. In the long run, this will affect the effectiveness of university education.
The university attaches great importance to the letter of advice from the students, and has deployed a rectification plan throughout the university.
All teaching units are required to organize teachers to carry out seminars and self-examinations for the final examinations, establish the examination and related systems, strengthen the supervision and inspection of the examination and examination and review links, refine the evaluation of results, and strengthen the comprehensive examination of all links of course teaching.
Solving the problems of single type of test and repetition of questions from the mechanism is a topic that the university examinations need to face. Of course, it needs to introduce corresponding system, but the key is to do the work in daily life. Teachers need to have a comprehensive grasp of the teaching situation. They should not only master the teaching content, but also the students' learning situation. If they can master both, teaching and learning will be better connected, form a virtuous interaction, and the exam will be more in line with reality, so that the "students with excellent grades" and "students with poor grades" can get what they deserve.

Based on the imaging principle of diffuse reflection of laser TV, the light emitted by the laser light source does not directly enter the eyeball. The Yangtze River Main Channel Achieves Full Coverage of Water-based Green Comprehensive Service Areas - Xinhuanet
Xinhua News Agency, Wuhan, December 11 (Reporter Wang Xian) The Chongqing Fuling Water Green Comprehensive Service Area was completed and put into operation as scheduled on December 10. This is the second water green comprehensive service area officially put into operation in the upper reaches of the Yangtze River after the Sichuan Luzhou Water Green Comprehensive Service Area was put into operation on December 8.
Up to now, the water green comprehensive service areas have covered the upper, middle and lower reaches of the Yangtze River.
According to Qiu Jianhua, deputy director of the Yangtze River Transportation Management Bureau of the Ministry of Transport, previously, Hubei, Hunan, Jiangxi, Anhui, and Jiangsu have built and operated 10 water-based green comprehensive service areas in the middle and lower reaches of the Yangtze River, which have been recognized by the majority of the crew, and have played an important role in promoting the high-quality development of Yangtze River shipping.
The comprehensive green service areas on the water cover all areas, which is another milestone event in the construction of green shipping on the Yangtze River. The reception of pollutants from Yangtze River ships and shipping services have been significantly improved.
According to reports, the water green comprehensive service area is an important facility for providing comprehensive production and living services for ships and crew members, and helping crew members solve "urgent and difficult" problems.
The two water-based green comprehensive service areas built and put into operation in the upper reaches of the Yangtze River integrate living services, production services and government services, and are themed on green environmental protection, with ship maintenance and ship emergency rescue services as their characteristics. They realize the "one-time berthing, multiple services" for ships in the port, and will make the work of crew members who sail the Yangtze River year-round more efficient and their lives more convenient.

Meta (formerly Facebook) is essentially an advertising company, and its revenue mainly comes from advertising. Today, a profitable advertising company has its own traffic channels.
Facebook, Instagram, Whatsapp, etc., no matter what kind of function positioning, are Meta’s traffic channels.
Meta is also the largest “advertising alliance”. Many Apps are connected to Meta’s advertising, and they are monetized through Meta’s advertising platform.
But times have changed.
First, because of the adjustment of Apple's privacy policy, Meta has difficulty in obtaining user data information, which affects the efficiency of advertising.
Apple's current privacy policy is very user-friendly, and will clearly ask if you want to allow an app to access your information, and most people's normal reaction is to say no.
If Google pulls another stunt, the impact on Meta's revenue could be even greater.
Second, the China factor in Meta’s previous revenue growth has also weakened (the proportion of this factor is uncertain, but it does exist).
Everyone knows that Meta doesn't have any business in China, but this understanding is not accurate.
At least 10% of Meta's revenue comes from China each year, mainly from companies that do business in China.
Meta has about hundreds of employees in its Hong Kong office, and several major agents in the mainland.

Facebook is getting older and new platforms, including TT, are not in Meta's hands.
